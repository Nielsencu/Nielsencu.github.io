Overview

Following mimic-video, here we fine tuned the massive video backbone model using 2B-parameter Cosmos-Predict2 on our own dataset. We realized that LoRA fine-tuning is necessary since the dynamics of the pretrained Cosmos video model does not generalize well to our dataset. Then, we train a lightweight action decoder on the latent representations of the fine-tuned video model to generate the action chunk. Each task, e.g. pushing a polyhedron or a cube, in a straight line or around an obstacle, is encoded as a prompt specified in natural language.
