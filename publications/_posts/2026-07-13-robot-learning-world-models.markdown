LoRA fine-tuning of a 2B-parameter Cosmos-Predict2 video backbone on our own robot manipulation dataset, with a lightweight action decoder trained on the fine-tuned model's latents to produce action chunks. Tasks (e.g. pushing a cube or polyhedron, straight or around an obstacle) are specified as natural-language prompts. Awarded 1st place in the World Models track of the ETH Zurich Robot Learning course and presented at the Vision and Robotics for Embodied AI (VREAI) event at ETH Zurich RobotX hall.
